## Posting + boosting steps (from TikTok's rules — follow exactly)
1. Post the video organically.
2. **Turn the content disclosure toggle ON** for the post (promoting your own product). Undisclosed marketing is itself ineligible — this one is easy to miss and it matters.
3. **Add a music track inside TikTok's editor from the Commercial Music Library** (or Unlimited Sounds) — that is the attention layer, and it is the only music Promote allows. Do NOT attach an ordinary trending song: Promote is unavailable for videos with uncleared audio.
4. Caption + hashtags are fine and expected. A chosen cover frame is fine.
5. Run Promote with the website objective and enter the **final Gumroad product-page URL directly** (no shorteners/redirects). The clickable button comes from Promote — that's why the video carries no link or text. The landing page's product and price must match what the post shows.
6. Rob wants this pushed through fast.
